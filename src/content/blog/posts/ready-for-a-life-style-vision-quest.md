What are you looking for in life? What is your inner voice telling you? Are you living the logistics or embracing the journey in full?

Each and every one of us has the birthright to a unique life mission, a potential with boundless possibilities. This promise starts in how you connect with yourself and the world around you. When you tend to look away, give away or follow suggestions made by commerce, politics or what you think others think of you, it might be time take a look inside and do a reality check, using the love and wisdom of the inner sanctuary we all have within.

**1. Enhance your Potential**

The reasons for doing a lifestyle vision quest are as personal as one’s fingerprint. It will however at least do one thing: it will lead you to yourPotential as it is ready and waiting for you right now.

**2. Become aware of your Freedom of Choice**

Do you find your life journey is often conditioned by what happens in the world? Or do you resonate with your personal mission and embrace your inner voice? A vision quest helps disclose the great many options to choose from between those two.

**3. Co-create your Life- Journey**

Is life something that happens to you or are you the co-creator? In a vision quest imagination, intuition and inspiration, the three layers of your being in body, mind and spirit,are especially vivid and as always ready to give you a peek in what is possible.

**4. Keep Balance**

A vision quest in its true form is a reality check and mindful way maintain balance **;** to reflect on the questionif your patterns are in sync with your mission.

**5** . **Find your true self**

A Vision Quest opens portals to the core being of who you truly are.

**6.** **Communicate better with yourself and your and the world around you.**

A life vision quest helps you distinguish fact from fiction, reality from illusion, make-believe from genuine and agenda’s from unintentional misunderstandings.It was William James, (1842-1910) founding father of psychotherapy in the U.S who said:

> _Whenever two people meet, there are really six people present. There is each man as he sees himself, each man as the other person sees him, and each man as he really is._

**7. Be content with what is.**

Rather than estimating you need something else first before you can do what you think you need to do, a vision quest shows you how all you need is already there and how life is already complete as it is under the given circumstances **.**

**8. Find** **Fulfillment.**

While it is often seen as a way of correcting a wrong, Karma in its essence is a destination. It’s up to up to the challenges, insights, gifts and tools of every unique individual how to position themselves in what happens in life. The secret is to have the inner voice have its say, while using the brain compass.

**9. Be Connected to yourself and the world around you in a wholesome way.**

The way you connect to the world begins with the way you connect with yourself. A life style vision quest helps you explore what resonates for you.
