Sunburns can be a summer bummer!

Summer invites us into nature, travel, and adventure but it can also bring along a few unwelcome discomforts. From sunburns to insect bites, motion sickness to summer colds, homeopathy offers gentle, effective support that’s easy to carry on the go. With remedies rooted in centuries of practice, you can build a compact kit that supports the whole family naturally.

Here are some summer essentials that The Hague Natural Health Centre thinks would make great travel buddies:

### **☀️** Sunburn – _Cantharis_

For sunburns that are red, raw, and intensely painful—especially if blisters form— **Cantharis 30C** is a top remedy. It soothes the burning sensation and can help speed up skin recovery. Think of it when the skin feels better with cool applications and worse with touch or heat.

**Tip:** Take it every few hours, then reduce as symptoms improve. Always pair it with rest, hydration, and gentle after-sun care. Fresh Aloe Vera is always helpful to apply directly on the skin as well.

### **🐝** Bee Stings & Insect Bites – _Apis mellifica_ and _Ledum palustre_

- **Apis 30C** is best for stings or bites that are **red, hot, swollen** , and **burning** —like classic bee or wasp stings. Relief comes from **cold applications** . The keynote for this remedy is oedema like swelling.
- **Ledum 30C** is ideal when the area is **puffy, cold, bruised** , or feels better with cold—great for mosquito bites, tick bites, or when a bite has a puncture-like quality similar to a needle.

**Tip:** Take the indicated remedy every 15–30 minutes at first, spacing doses as the swelling and discomfort ease.

### **🕷** Tick Bites – _Ledum palustre_

Ledum is also the first remedy to reach for after a tick bite, especially if the area looks bluish or swollen. It may help reduce inflammation and support the body’s immune response.

**Tip:** Use Ledum in 200c potency twice a day on the day of the tick bite and repeat for 2-3 days. Even with Ledum, always clean the bite area thoroughly and monitor for signs of infection or rash. Consult a healthcare provider promptly if symptoms arise.

### **🤧** Summer Colds – _Ferrum phosphoricum_

Summer colds often sneak up after travel, air conditioning, or overexposure to sun and wind. When symptoms are just starting—mild fever, sore throat, fatigue— **Ferrum phos 6X or 30C** can help nip it in the bud.

**Tip:** Use at the very first sign of illness, and rest as much as possible. If it progresses to a more defined stage, another remedy might be better suited.

### **🤢** Travel Tummy & Motion Sickness – _Nux vomica_ and _Cocculus indicus_

- **Nux vomica 30C** is excellent for digestive upsets from overeating, rich food, or alcohol—think nausea, cramping, and irritability.
- **Cocculus 30C** is your go-to for **motion sickness** , especially when dizziness or queasiness are worsened by travel, lack of sleep, or caring for others.

**Tip:** Take before and during travel, or at the first signs of nausea. Repeat as needed, but stop once symptoms ease.

### **✈️** How to Pack Your Homeopathy Travel Kit

Keep it simple. A few versatile remedies in 30C potency can go a long way. Store them in a cool, dry place and label them clearly. If you’re flying, a small kit with 6–10 remedies easily fits in your carry-on.

**Starter kit suggestions:** Cantharis · Apis · Ledum · Ferrum phos · Nux vomica · Cocculus · Arnica (for bumps/bruises) · Aconite (for shock/fright) · Belladonna (heat/sun-related fevers)

Homeopathy offers more than symptom relief—it’s a mindful way to connect with the body’s natural rhythm and healing power. With remedies like those ones noted above, your summer adventures can be just a bit smoother, more comfortable, and fully present.
