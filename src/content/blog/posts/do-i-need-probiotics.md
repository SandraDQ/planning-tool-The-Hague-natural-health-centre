Should I Add a Probiotic to My Supplement Routine?

Probiotics can be beneficial, but not all are created equal. Some even contain hidden

sugars, which can hinder progress. Before jumping into probiotics, it’s essential to

take a more holistic approach. This is where the 5R protocol comes in—a step-by-

step method to restore digestive health used in both functional medicine and

Systematic Kinesiology.

The 5R Approach to Digestive Health

The 5R protocol is a practical framework for getting your gut health back on track.

Let’s explore each stage:

1. Remove

Start by eliminating anything causing gut irritation. This could include specific foods,

harmful bacteria, or infections. Using Kinesiology, we can pinpoint exactly what needs to be removed—whether it’s cutting out certain foods like dairy or addressing an overgrowth of yeast like Candida.

2. Replace

If your body isn’t producing enough digestive enzymes or stomach acid, your digestion can struggle. Replacing what’s missing is key for absorbing nutrients effectively. Kinesiology helps determine which supplements or enzymes your body needs to function smoothly.

3. Reinoculate

Once your gut is clear and supported, it’s time to introduce **probiotics**. However, probiotics are most effective after the first two steps are complete. We can identify the right strains tailored to your body’s needs, as each strain has a different role.

4. Repair

After balancing digestion, you may need to heal the gut lining, especially if you suffer from "leaky gut." This involves strengthening the gut barrier through the right nutrition and supplements, which Kinesiology can help identify.

5. Rebalance

Finally, maintaining balance is crucial. Continue with healthy habits like managing stress, staying active, and monitoring trigger foods. With a balanced gut, your body may even tolerate foods that once caused discomfort—but it’s always best to test these changes with Kinesiology.

The Takeaway

Probiotics can be an excellent tool for gut health, but they work best as part of a larger plan. Addressing gut irritants and supporting digestion first is key. By following the **5R protocol**, probiotics can make a real difference in how you feel.
