To co-create a healthier, happier community, using natural sustainable therapies, helping you to live with more confidence, ease and purpose, and less symptoms, pain and reliance on long term medications.

## Healthy Communities

How do you define health? Technically speaking, this is the absence of dis-ease in the body. Homeopathically, we define health as the ability to adapt to change. This means that if you experience major changes in life like a move to another country, a new job, death in the family, etc. - an impactful event happens, then you can go thru the process of adapting and changing without getting stuck in the emotion. When we are in good health mentally and physically, we can adapt to changes in a positive way despite the challenges we face.

## Education

In ancient times, we were more connected to our world (Earth / Patcha Mama) and intuitively understood which plants and minerals in our environment could help us heal. We've lost this connection not only to nature but to ourselves. Our goal at the health centre is to help people find the tools to reconnect to nature themselves. In this way, they become more empowered to take control of their own health instead of completely relying out others to tell them what to do.
