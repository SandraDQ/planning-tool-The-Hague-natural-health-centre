## Ayurveda

Ayurveda literally means the science of life. It is an ancient healing system that originated in India thousands of years ago, which emphasises the need for balance in all areas, mind, body and soul. Ayurveda is focused on lifestyle, it uses natural remedies, including herbs, minerals, and other plant-based treatments, to address underlying root causes of illness and promote overall health, safely and gently.

## Gestalt Counselling

Gestalt Counselling is a gentle form of talking therapy that focuses on the here and now. The aim is to give people clarity and feel better. It allows people to work through allaspectsof their situation, and gradually focuses on self-awareness and taking action in the present moment. It can help people overcome grief, depression and rumination on past events, procrastination, or anxiety.

## Kinesiology

Systemic kinesiology is a holistic health practice that uses muscle testing to identify and correct imbalances in the body. It combines techniques like nutrition, massage, and acupressure to enhance natural healing. Benefits include reduced stress, increased energy, and relief from chronic pain. By addressing the root causes of issues, Kinesiology supports overall well-being.

## High Power Laser Therapy

High Power Laser therapy uses laser light to deliver energy directly to the cells. The light penetrates the mitochondria, where it is absorbed by light-sensitive molecules (chromophores). This stimulates cellular activity, increases ATP production (the cell's energy source), and enhances blood flow and oxygen supply to the tissue. Resulting in faster & more effective tissue healing, often in 1/3 of the typical recovery time.

## Mindfulness Stress Reduction

Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) is an evidence-based program that teaches mindfulness meditation to help manage stress and improve overall well-being. It encourages awareness of the present moment, reducing the impact of stress and anxiety. Benefits include enhanced emotional resilience, better focus, and improved mental health. Regular practice of MBSR can lead to a calmer mind and a more balanced life.

## Therapeutic Massage

Therapeutic massage focuses on relieving tension, reducing pain, and improving overall muscle function through targeted techniques. Regular therapeutic massage supports both physical and mental well-being.

## Foot Reflexology

Foot reflexology is a therapeutic practice based on the principle that specific points on the feet correspond to different organs and systems in the body. By applying pressure to these reflex points, a reflexologist aims to promote balance and healing. Benefits include improved circulation, stress relief, enhanced relaxation, pain reduction, better sleep, and improved overall health. It can also help alleviate symptoms like headaches, digestive issues, and anxiety.
