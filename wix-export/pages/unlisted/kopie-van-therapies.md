## Acupuncture

Acupuncture is part of Traditional Chinese Medicine, which has been practiced for over 5,000 years. Its core principle is to promote the free circulation of life energy, known as Qi. Through acupuncture, blockages in the energy pathways are resolved, allowing Qi to flow freely again. This treatment involves the use of gentle needles placed along the meridians of the body to increase the flow of energy.

## Chinese Herbalism

Traditional Chinese herbal therapy uses substances of plant, mineral, and animal origin. Along with acupuncture, it forms a core pillar of Chinese medicine. Its strength lies in carefully composed formulas, often containing 4 to 20 herbs, tailored to an individual’s specific imbalance. Unlike Western herbal medicine, single-herb treatments are rarely used. Today, around 600 different herbs are commonly applied in clinical practice.
